# Design spec

This repository maintains one product template in `tdd-optional/project`. Read its task-specific
testing policy and preserve the shared Claude Code/Codex skill meaning. Use existing authorization
and decisions. Historical experiments use their recorded Git revisions.

Read that product's `PROJECT-POLICY.md`, `templates/`, and
`examples/skills/design-spec/SKILL.md` with its relevant references. The
[adoption guide](../../../README.md) explains adoption and the maker/product boundary.
For maker-history documents, use the applicable form as a writing aid and root governance; do not
silently treat the maker repository as a product or invent a new approval requirement.

Follow that example to write requirements, acceptance criteria and linked design contracts. Resolve its repository-relative paths against that
product folder while authoring the template; in an adopted project they resolve against the product root.
Keep the actual request, required design inputs and evidence; do not fabricate acceptance or test results.
The user may already have authorized this stage. Questions and status requests do not trigger implementation.

The product forms and examples are the maintained sources. Do not add another embedded form here.
A plan change does not require manufacturing a RED result. Use TDD when selected for the work.
Scope changes update the affected authoritative documents.
